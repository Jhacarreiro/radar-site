1️⃣ **Make invisible workload visible**
Hours and deadlines miss the planning, emotional coordination and constant switching employees carry. Research links multitasking to substantial performance costs.

💡 Why it matters
Map where mental load sits, set core collaboration hours and assign cover. Flexible work without protected offline time is still always-on.

☕ Coffee talk
Who keeps the team's deadlines in their head, and what happens when that person stops?

---

2️⃣ **Fix activation before you chase distribution**
Gamma had about a year of runway left when it rebuilt around AI. The new product took daily sign-ups from hundreds to more than 100,000, with no paid marketing.

💡 Why it matters
The pivot worked because onboarding solved the blank-page problem. Find the first moment of value before spending to fill the funnel.

☕ Coffee talk
How much growth budget is compensating for an onboarding screen that still asks users to start from scratch?

---

3️⃣ **Give every robot pilot a narrow job**
MIT SMR says humanoid adoption will not follow GenAI's curve: hardware, software and connectivity needs vary by task. Current pilots still rely on controlled zones.

💡 Why it matters
Start with one workflow, then price safety, integration, maintenance and downtime. A demo is not an operating model.

☕ Coffee talk
Which shift task is worth a humanoid once the safety zone, downtime and integration bill arrive?
