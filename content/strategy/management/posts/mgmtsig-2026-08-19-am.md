1️⃣ **Rebuild roles around outcomes, not old functions**
A solo founder used AI to move from sketches to CAD files, vendor outreach and a working pre-order site without an engineering team.

💡 Why it matters
AI can collapse several handoffs into one role. Managers should redraw ownership before adding another specialist to the chain.

☕ Coffee talk
Which handoff in your launch process survives only because the old tools made it necessary?

---

2️⃣ **Recruit from a map, not a funnel**
Cursor's head of talent starts with a precise role scope, maps the people who could excel, then pursues them instead of waiting for applicants.

💡 Why it matters
A busy hiring funnel can hide weak targeting. Define the few people you want before optimising applicant volume.

☕ Coffee talk
Can your hiring manager name five people worth chasing, or just the number of open applications?

---

3️⃣ **Turn a setback into a point of view**
David Rubenstein built Carlyle around Washington expertise after losing his White House role and struggling to find work when the Carter administration ended.

💡 Why it matters
A constraint becomes strategy only when it changes where you compete. Generic resilience is not a positioning decision.

☕ Coffee talk
Which awkward piece of company history could tell you where the business has an unfair read on the market?
