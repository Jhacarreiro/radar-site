1️⃣ **Your AI draft is somebody else's reading assignment**
In a new essay, operator Colin Breck describes AI-written proposals that list changes but omit purpose and context. The recipient has to reconstruct the thinking the sender skipped.

💡 Why it matters
Require the author to state the decision needed and check the supporting evidence before circulating a generated proposal.

☕ Coffee talk
Can the proposal's owner explain the decision without sending you another generated summary?

---

2️⃣ **Check what the target makes people neglect**
The Goals Gone Wild research warns that narrow stretch targets can crowd out unmeasured work and encourage cheating. The authors support goals, but reject treating them as a universal management remedy.

💡 Why it matters
Review the target alongside the work it leaves unrewarded. Make quality checks explicit before attaching a bonus.

☕ Coffee talk
Which customer problem can your team ignore and still collect the full bonus?

---

3️⃣ **Put more than one workable deal on the table**
The MESO negotiation method offers different packages at once, each equally valuable to you. The other side's preference reveals which terms matter, rather than leaving price as the only issue.

💡 Why it matters
Score trade-offs before the meeting. Offer packages you can actually accept, then ask which is closest and why.

☕ Coffee talk
Have you priced the delivery deadline before offering another discount?
