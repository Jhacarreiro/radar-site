1️⃣ **Make priorities survive a direct comparison**
Three questions cut through a crowded list: defend doing A instead of B, name what cannot fail, and state the cost of delay. Priority labels alone avoid the trade-off.

💡 Why it matters
Force every new request to displace something visible. Otherwise the team gets a longer list, not a decision.

☕ Coffee talk
Which current priority would you struggle to defend against the one it pushed aside?

---

2️⃣ **Buying a company still beats searching forever**
Stanford's 2026 study puts the historical search-fund acquisition rate at 58%. Recent cohorts fell to about 48%, while the average successful search took 20 months.

💡 Why it matters
Back the operator and the search process, not just the target. Deal discipline matters more when competition extends the hunt.

☕ Coffee talk
At month 18, is patience still discipline or has the searcher started needing a deal?

---

3️⃣ **Replace the AI spec with a test**
Anthropic's product team treats evals as the main artifact for model work. Product managers inspect failures, turn them into test cases and use the scores to guide iteration.

💡 Why it matters
A PRD describes intended behaviour. An eval exposes whether the system can deliver it, and catches regressions when models change.

☕ Coffee talk
How many AI requirements would survive being rewritten as pass-or-fail examples?
