1️⃣ **Train managers before they inherit a team**
AMA research found that 51% of managers entered people management without formal leadership training. Only 27% of employees rated their manager highly effective.

💡 Why it matters
Prepare people before promotion, then give them time to coach. A title does not teach anyone how to develop a team.

☕ Coffee talk
Who approved the promotion when the new manager had never practised a difficult feedback conversation?

---

2️⃣ **Make managers the AI adoption layer**
Gallup found that 79% of employees use AI frequently when managers actively support it, versus 46% without that support. Yet only 21% say their manager does so.

💡 Why it matters
Train managers on live team workflows. Tool access and a policy document will not turn AI into normal operating practice.

☕ Coffee talk
Who owns that 33-point adoption gap: the AI team, HR or the manager in the weekly check-in?

---

3️⃣ **Run the core and the future on different clocks**
Bain found only 52% of CEOs have the routines to run today's business and change it at the same time. Delivery and development need different decision cycles and metrics.

💡 Why it matters
Separate operating reviews from growth-bet reviews. One cadence will either starve the future or loosen accountability in the core.

☕ Coffee talk
Which future bet is still being judged by this quarter's operating dashboard?
