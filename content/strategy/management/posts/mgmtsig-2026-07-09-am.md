1️⃣ **Leadership moves into workflow**
Harvard Business Impact's 2026 study says 50% of organizations are expanding AI-based talent management and internal mobility; 53% expect leaders to use more AI in strategic decisions.

💡 Why it matters
Leadership development has to move closer to real decisions, not sit in a calendar of courses.

☕ Coffee talk
Who is checking whether leaders use AI to decide better, or just to decide faster?

---

2️⃣ **Process is now a design choice**
Gusto's CTO says a five-person team built a new product line in 10 weeks with Claude Code, a permanent Zoom room and almost no standard process: no Jira, Figma, specs or standups.

💡 Why it matters
When AI absorbs more building work, coordination overhead becomes a management variable, not a law of nature.

☕ Coffee talk
Which meetings survive once a PR can be the proposal?

---

3️⃣ **PLG sales needs clean measurement**
First Round's Supabase episode says the company turned product-led signals into a sales motion paid only on incremental uplift over a control group.

💡 Why it matters
Sales can help self-serve growth, but only if leaders measure lift instead of letting reps harvest demand the product already created.

☕ Coffee talk
How many PLG teams know what sales added, instead of what sales touched?
