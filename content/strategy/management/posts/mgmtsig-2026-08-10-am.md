1️⃣ **Treat every important hire like an executive search**
Cursor's talent lead rejects the usual recruiting funnel. His alternative is simple: define exceptional, map the few people who fit, then pursue them properly.

💡 Why it matters
Hiring speed is a weak metric if the process starts with whoever happens to apply. The candidate map sets the ceiling.

☕ Coffee talk
How many open roles have a named list of exceptional people before the job ad goes live?

---

2️⃣ **Alignment is not agreement**
In one executive team, 10 of 13 leaders said the transformation was clear. Asked to write down what would change, their answers pointed in different directions.

💡 Why it matters
Vague consensus pushes unresolved trade-offs into delivery teams, where they become extra initiatives and lost time.

☕ Coffee talk
Would your top team write the same answer to "what are we not changing"?

---

3️⃣ **Gratitude travels down the org chart**
Across three studies, managers who received grateful messages were more likely to thank their own reports. Those employees then repeated the behavior.

💡 Why it matters
Leader behavior changes what people notice. Specific, timely thanks can spread without another culture programme.

☕ Coffee talk
Who was the last person your managers thanked in public, and for what exactly?
