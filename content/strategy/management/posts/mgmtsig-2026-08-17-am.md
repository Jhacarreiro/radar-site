1️⃣ **Design less before you design faster**
OpenAI's head of product design tells his team to ask whether a problem needs new pixels at all. Model behaviour or an existing component may solve it better.

💡 Why it matters
AI speeds up output, including unnecessary output. The senior move is to remove the interface before polishing it.

☕ Coffee talk
Which screen in your roadmap exists because nobody asked whether the model could handle it?

---

2️⃣ **Stop staffing product teams by ratio**
Whatnot uses roughly 20 to 22 product managers and does not assign one to every engineering team. Senior PM leaders still spend most of their time doing IC work.

💡 Why it matters
A fixed PM-to-engineer ratio creates coordination jobs. Add a PM when the problem needs one, not because the org chart has an empty box.

☕ Coffee talk
How many PM roles in your plan solve a customer problem rather than a staffing convention?

---

3️⃣ **Judge the decision before you judge the result**
Six-time CEO Mike Grossman argues that luck shapes outcomes more than leaders admit. His test is the quality of the inputs and choices available at the time.

💡 Why it matters
Outcome bias rewards bad calls that worked and punishes sound calls that met bad luck. Review the process while the evidence is still visible.

☕ Coffee talk
Would your last post-mortem praise the same decision if the quarter had ended differently?
