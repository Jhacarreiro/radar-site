1️⃣ **Delegate the work without disappearing**
CCL's delegation framework pairs authority with support and agreed review points. IMD adds a useful constraint: adjust your involvement to the person's capability and the task's stakes.

💡 Why it matters
Agree what needs review before handing work over. A checkpoint should catch trouble without making every step wait for you.

☕ Coffee talk
Which decision can your team actually make before the next checkpoint?

---

2️⃣ **Put the break in the calendar**
In Microsoft's 2021 study of 14 people, stress-related brain activity built up across back-to-back video meetings. With 10-minute meditation breaks, it stayed steadier.

💡 Why it matters
Shorten the default meeting slot and protect the gap. This small study supports testing breaks, not a universal recovery time.

☕ Coffee talk
Does the gap between your calls survive the first meeting running over?

---

3️⃣ **Price the exit option from your rival's side**
Strategy research describes a trade-off: resources you can move elsewhere preserve flexibility, but weaken commitment. INSEAD's model shows how that can hurt against committed rivals in winner-take-all markets.

💡 Why it matters
Before entering a market, test how a focused rival would respond to your ability to retreat. A bigger budget may not settle the contest.

☕ Coffee talk
Why would the incumbent back down when it knows you can redeploy the team?
