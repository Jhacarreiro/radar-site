1️⃣ **AI savings need a manager**
BCG's 2026 AI at Work survey says 74% of frontline employees now use AI regularly and 42% of those users save at least a full workday a week, but 66% get little or no guidance on where that time should go.

💡 Why it matters
Tool rollout is no longer the bottleneck. The management job is deciding which work disappears, which work gets upgraded and who owns the freed capacity.

☕ Coffee talk
Who is brave enough to put the saved Friday on a real operating plan?

---

2️⃣ **Mid-hold needs fresh energy**
Bain's 2026 Private Equity Midyear Report warns that value creation often gets lost in the middle of longer holding periods, just as management teams are tired and incentives have gone stale.

💡 Why it matters
The useful lesson is not PE-specific. Any owner sitting on a mature plan has to refresh talent, incentives and priorities before drift becomes the strategy.

☕ Coffee talk
How many teams are still executing the plan that made sense two shocks ago?

---

3️⃣ **Secrecy can unblock innovation**
INSEAD research on more than 47,000 US hydraulic fracturing wells found stronger trade secret protection increased secret ingredient use and did not reduce disclosed innovation or patenting.

💡 Why it matters
Leaders treat IP as legal housekeeping too often. In high-leakage businesses, protection can decide whether an invention leaves the lab or stays unused.

☕ Coffee talk
Which useful idea is still parked because nobody trusts the handoff?
