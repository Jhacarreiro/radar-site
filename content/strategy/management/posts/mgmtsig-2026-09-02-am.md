1️⃣ **Build networks into leadership development**
MIT SMR argues that high performers need more than harder assignments. They also need trust, visibility and advocates beyond their immediate team.

💡 Why it matters
Give each succession candidate a cross-functional problem and a sponsor. Performance that stays inside one reporting line is easy to miss.

☕ Coffee talk
Who knows your strongest manager's work outside the people already rating it?

---

2️⃣ **Stop making pay depend on who feels safe asking**
Stanford research found that lower-class employees negotiate less and face a bigger hiring penalty when they do. Asking is an uneven pay process.

💡 Why it matters
Publish salary ranges and promotion criteria. Managers should explain the offer before confidence, background or bargaining style sets the number.

☕ Coffee talk
How much of your pay gap is just a confidence test nobody admits running?

---

3️⃣ **Separate the maker from the critic**
Anshu Chimala uses one AI agent to build and a fresh, stronger model to judge screenshots against a fixed quality bar. The critic sees the result, not the effort.

💡 Why it matters
Use independent review for subjective work. Clear references and stop criteria beat asking the same system to mark its own homework.

☕ Coffee talk
Would the work still look finished to a reviewer who never saw the prompt?
