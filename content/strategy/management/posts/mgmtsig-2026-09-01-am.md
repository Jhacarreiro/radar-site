1️⃣ **Delay the concession after a good story**
Two experiments with 622 B2B salespeople found that a buyer's story raised willingness to concede by 17% and trust by 10%, even when the buyer had lied.

💡 Why it matters
Separate persuasion from verification. No price or term decision should be made while the story is still doing the work.

☕ Coffee talk
Who checks the buyer's facts while your best negotiator is enjoying the anecdote?

---

2️⃣ **Demand value that reaches the P&L**
BCG warns that AI pilots can report productivity gains without producing cost savings or measurable business value. Local efficiency is not an end-to-end result.

💡 Why it matters
Give every pilot an operating metric, an owner for the full workflow and a date for stopping it. Usage is not a return.

☕ Coffee talk
How many successful pilots could finance themselves if the central budget vanished tomorrow?

---

3️⃣ **Do not mistake the CRM for the sales process**
Jen Abel maps enterprise sales as roughly 15 steps, not the five stages shown in most CRMs. The pipeline forecasts the deal; it does not tell the team how to move it.

💡 Why it matters
Manage the work between stages: stakeholder access, pilot design, procurement and redlines. That is where momentum is usually lost.

☕ Coffee talk
Which stalled deal is still green because the CRM has no box for the person blocking it?
