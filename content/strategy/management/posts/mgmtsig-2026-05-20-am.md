1️⃣ **Feedback is a risk decision**
Harvard Business School research says managers sometimes inflate weak reviews because punishment can trigger retaliation. Small slip-ups may cost less than the drama they create.

💡 Why it matters
Performance systems need judgment, not automatic candor. The real question is which problems deserve correction, pay consequences or quiet coaching.

☕ Coffee talk
Which review is pretending to be honest while everyone prices in the backlash?

---

2️⃣ **AI change dies in false alignment**
BCG says only 5% of companies get substantial value from AI, and argues the failure is usually human: unclear goals, weak buy-in and leaders agreeing too early.

💡 Why it matters
AI programmes need fewer use cases and sharper trade-offs. Six pilots with no owner are not strategy.

☕ Coffee talk
Who has actually agreed what AI is meant to change, besides the slide title?

---

3️⃣ **Leadership starts with follower needs**
HBR argues that employees trust leaders less despite record leadership spending. The better test is whether leaders understand what followers need in the moment.

💡 Why it matters
Charisma and authenticity are poor substitutes for fit. Teams follow when leadership matches the work, pressure and trust gap in front of them.

☕ Coffee talk
Which leader is still selling their style while the team needs something else?
