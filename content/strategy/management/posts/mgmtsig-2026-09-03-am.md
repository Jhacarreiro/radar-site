1️⃣ **Name the anxiety before launching the transformation**
A four-year study found that unspoken leadership anxiety can turn a plausible change plan into busywork, blame and ritual while the operating model stays put.

💡 Why it matters
Before approving the vision, ask what evidence could disprove it and what leaders themselves must change. Confidence is not a control.

☕ Coffee talk
Which transformation meeting would become awkward if the CEO admitted what is actually worrying them?

---

2️⃣ **Choose whether you want more ideas or better ones**
Research tracking 2,376 inventor-year observations found that rewards pushed specialists towards more inventive patents, while generalists produced more patents with lower novelty.

💡 Why it matters
Separate rewards for volume from rewards for impact. One incentive will not produce both outcomes from every kind of expert.

☕ Coffee talk
Does your innovation bonus pay for a useful patent or merely another submission before quarter-end?

---

3️⃣ **Give every agent a narrow job and an audit trail**
Claire Vo runs about 30 active agents across inboxes, code review, compliance and support. The useful pattern is bounded ownership, not one bot pretending to run everything.

💡 Why it matters
Assign each agent a named process, permissions and review rule. Autonomy without a visible handoff leaves managers accountable for work they cannot inspect.

☕ Coffee talk
Which agent can act today without anyone being able to explain its last decision?
