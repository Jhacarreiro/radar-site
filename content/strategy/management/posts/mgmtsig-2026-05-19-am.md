1️⃣ **HQ is not the market**
HBR says global companies still let proximity to headquarters shape strategy: regional leaders often wake up to decisions they had no chance to influence.

💡 Why it matters
This is not a timezone nuisance. It is a decision-rights problem that quietly prices local expertise below room attendance.

☕ Coffee talk
Which market insight dies first because the right person was asleep?

---

2️⃣ **Transformation fails in the middle**
BCG says only one in four growth transformations succeeds, and argues that CEOs overrate the launch while underrating employee agency and execution discipline.

💡 Why it matters
Change needs governance after the memo. The weak point is usually belief without buy-in, then fatigue without closure.

☕ Coffee talk
Who is still calling it alignment because nobody objected in the meeting?

---

3️⃣ **AI is cutting the lower rungs**
INSEAD frames AI workforce planning around reskill, rehire and recreate, with a sharper warning: entry-level pipelines may need deliberate protection.

💡 Why it matters
Automating junior work can save time now and destroy the apprenticeship system later. Skills do not appear when software arrives.

☕ Coffee talk
Where do tomorrow's operators learn judgment if today's first jobs disappear?
