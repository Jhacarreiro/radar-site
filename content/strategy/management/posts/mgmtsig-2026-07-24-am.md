1️⃣ **Step up early, then get out of the way**
MIT Sloan's Step Up, Step Back model puts leaders deep in the opening of a change: set the case, priorities and boundaries, then retreat enough for teams to own delivery.

💡 Why it matters
Define what cannot drift before execution starts. During delivery, review outcomes and obstacles without taking the work back.

☕ Coffee talk
Can the sponsor leave the room without the transformation waiting for another instruction?

---

2️⃣ **Quiet is a team performance tool**
A survey of more than 6,000 knowledge workers found only one of 20-plus office amenities consistently marked exceptional teams: quiet space. Members were 52% more likely to have it.

💡 Why it matters
Protect 60-minute focus blocks, cut status interruptions and let people match the workspace to the task.

☕ Coffee talk
Could anyone use a 60-minute focus block here without defending it from meetings and messages?

---

3️⃣ **Hire people who can see the whole workflow**
Netflix CPTO Elizabeth Stone now prioritizes systems thinking over narrow specialization: seeing how work connects, while judging where AI helps and where it adds noise.

💡 Why it matters
Interview for downstream judgment. Ask candidates to trace how one local improvement changes customers, controls and other teams.

☕ Coffee talk
Can your best specialist explain what breaks downstream when AI speeds up one part of the workflow?
