1️⃣ **AI makes analysis faster. Judgment is now the constraint**
AI can speed up research and scenario work, but leaders still need time to challenge assumptions, weigh trade-offs and own strategic decisions.

💡 Why it matters
Faster analysis only helps if decision rights and review capacity keep pace.

☕ Coffee talk
Who gets the time to challenge the AI-shaped recommendation before it becomes the plan?

---

2️⃣ **AI use can weaken the team's map of who knows what**
A new study reports that treating AI as a teammate can erode trust and coordination between colleagues, even as people see one another's expertise more clearly.

💡 Why it matters
If routine questions go to a bot first, teams may lose the informal knowledge-sharing that helps them coordinate under pressure.

☕ Coffee talk
Which expertise on the team is becoming invisible because everyone asks the assistant first?

---

3️⃣ **The CEO cannot delegate ownership of strategy**
McKinsey argues that CEOs must personally steer the value-creation thesis, resource reallocations and the pace of strategic change, even when strategy is developed across the leadership team.

💡 Why it matters
The team can build the plan; the CEO still owns its hard choices and follow-through.

☕ Coffee talk
Which resource move is waiting for the CEO to stop treating it as someone else's call?
