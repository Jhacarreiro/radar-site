1️⃣ **Make exploration a separate mode**
MIT SMR tested an exploration-based search tool with 349 people. It produced ideas rated 14% more creative in one study and 11% more creative in another.

💡 Why it matters
Do not use the same AI setup for retrieval and invention. Give early-stage teams a deliberate exploration mode that surfaces uncommon inputs, then bring efficiency back for execution.

☕ Coffee talk
How many teams are calling it ideation while asking the same model for the most likely answer?

---

2️⃣ **Manage enterprise sales beyond the CRM stages**
Jen Abel maps a large enterprise deal as roughly 15 steps, not the five stages shown in most CRMs. The work starts before the demo and continues through the pilot, procurement and redlines.

💡 Why it matters
Manage the actual sequence, owners and next evidence required. A tidy pipeline can hide a deal that has not advanced with the buyer at all.

☕ Coffee talk
Which of your "late-stage" deals has still not agreed what a successful pilot means?

---

3️⃣ **Give the first specialist room to decide**
Lovable's first designer says the founders let him shape product and brand with unusual autonomy. The initial identity took 30 minutes; user interviews, repeated rebuilds and clear two-way-door decisions did the rest.

💡 Why it matters
Early specialists need a decision boundary, not a committee. Keep feedback close to users and reserve escalation for choices that are costly to reverse.

☕ Coffee talk
Would your fourth employee have been allowed to make that 30-minute call?
