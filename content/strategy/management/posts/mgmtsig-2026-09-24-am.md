1️⃣ **Give the new hire a route to useful work**
Microsoft's remote-onboarding research recommends a named buddy and, for developers, a simple first task to learn the workflow. Documents alone do not explain how the team gets work done.

💡 Why it matters
Set an initial deliverable and name who can unblock it. Check role expectations with the manager, not just HR.

☕ Coffee talk
Can your newest hire finish a small piece of work without having to discover the organisation first?

---

2️⃣ **Write the failure story before kickoff**
Gary Klein's premortem asks a team to assume the project has failed and explain why. McKinsey's account then turns those vulnerabilities into changes to the plan.

💡 Why it matters
Give the highest-priority risks an owner and a mitigation deadline before committing to the launch plan.

☕ Coffee talk
Which launch dependency still has nobody responsible for checking that it will hold?

---

3️⃣ **Keep the estimates made before everyone agreed**
In a 2011 experiment, seeing other people's estimates narrowed the spread of answers and raised confidence without improving group accuracy. Agreement was a poor test of correctness.

💡 Why it matters
Collect forecasts privately before discussion. Keep that baseline so later convergence has to be explained.

☕ Coffee talk
Did the sales forecast improve after the meeting, or did everyone just move towards the same number?
