1️⃣ **Manager ambition needs manager capability**
AMA's survey of 1,000-plus professionals found that 87% feel confident about advancement, but only 27% rate their manager highly effective. Some 51% entered people management without formal training.

💡 Why it matters
Career paths do little if managers cannot coach, assign stretch work or turn learning into daily practice.

☕ Coffee talk
How many new managers got a team before they got any preparation?

---

2️⃣ **Productivity can hide a workload tax**
Lenny's 2026 tech survey found 82% are more productive with AI. Yet 51% fear more work for the same pay, while significant burnout rose from 44.7% to 55.7% in a year.

💡 Why it matters
Leaders must decide where saved time goes. Otherwise every efficiency gain quietly becomes a higher baseline.

☕ Coffee talk
Is the team saving time, or has the target already swallowed it?

---

3️⃣ **Sustainability needs a business owner**
Colgate-Palmolive's chief sustainability officer told BCG that sustainability strategy must tie directly to business outcomes and company priorities.

💡 Why it matters
Tie each initiative to an owner and an operating target that can survive the next portfolio review.

☕ Coffee talk
Would this initiative survive the budget review without the word sustainability?
