1️⃣ **The CEO calendar is part of the operating model**
Bain found that only 41% of surveyed CEOs protect time for deep work. Routine operations keep displacing the decisions only the CEO can make.

💡 Why it matters
Delegation fails when the CEO remains the default escalation path. The calendar shows whether decision rights work below the top.

☕ Coffee talk
Which meeting on Monday exists because someone below the CEO still cannot make the call?

---

2️⃣ **Give founder communication a job**
Lenny's Newsletter maps founder communication to three audience outcomes: identification, trust or belief in a future. Each needs different messages and channels.

💡 Why it matters
Posting more is not a strategy. Decide whose behaviour should change, then choose the story, format and cadence that serve it.

☕ Coffee talk
Does your founder's latest post help a customer decide, or just prove the founder was online?

---

3️⃣ **Focus is credible only when something good gets cut**
MIT Sloan is ending its Management Review after nearly 70 years to centralise communications, despite the publication's reputation and a public backlash.

💡 Why it matters
Strategic focus is tested on respected activities, not obvious failures. Keeping every good asset is how priorities become slogans.

☕ Coffee talk
Which admired programme survives in your company because nobody wants their name on the closure note?
