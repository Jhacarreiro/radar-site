1️⃣ **The chair's job ends short of running the company**
INSEAD's latest governance note puts the chair in charge of the board's process. Supporting the CEO should preserve independent judgement, without taking over executive work.

💡 Why it matters
Review where the chair intervenes in operations. Advice can quietly become a second chain of command.

☕ Coffee talk
Can the board assess the CEO fairly after the chair has been directing the management team?

---

2️⃣ **Make the next funding decision before the money is gone**
Bain's agile budgeting framework starts innovation investments small and reallocates funding as results emerge. Stable product teams can continue while low-value initiatives stop.

💡 Why it matters
Set the next evidence review when approving funding. A delivery status report is not evidence that the investment still earns its place.

☕ Coffee talk
Which initiative could lose funding at the next review, and does its sponsor know the criteria?

---

3️⃣ **Put a limit on unfinished work**
Kanban guidance limits work already started but not finished, with new work pulled when capacity is available. Task-switching research also shows that moving between tasks costs time.

💡 Why it matters
Cap concurrent work and track how long open items wait. Help clear blocked tasks before assigning another priority.

☕ Coffee talk
How many more tasks will you start while the same items sit in the review queue?
