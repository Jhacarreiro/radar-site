1️⃣ **AI adoption stalls in the invisible work**
A two-year study found a law firm with AI sandboxes and training ended with three organization-wide solutions; a better-supported medical centre had 141.

💡 Why it matters
Teams need time, technical help and credit to test, review and scale solutions. Training and tool access do not cover that work.

☕ Coffee talk
Who has the time and credit to keep your AI pilot alive after the demo?

---

2️⃣ **The board chair is not a second CEO**
INSEAD says the chair's job is to shape how the board works, not to run the company. A 2018 INSEAD study interviewed 140 board stakeholders and surveyed 200 chairs.

💡 Why it matters
A chair should set the agenda, make room for challenge and keep the CEO relationship clear. Taking the operating wheel weakens both roles.

☕ Coffee talk
At the last board meeting, who set the terms of the debate before the CEO spoke?

---

3️⃣ **Shared leadership still needs coordination**
A September study of 58 hackathon teams found that shared leadership alone did not guarantee performance. Fragmentation hurt when leaders were far apart; coordination made geography less important.

💡 Why it matters
For a temporary team under time pressure, name how its leaders will coordinate and connect subgroups before the work starts.

☕ Coffee talk
In your next sprint, who owns the handoff between the two clusters?
