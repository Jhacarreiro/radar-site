1️⃣ **Do not hire the fastest inbox**
Across 11.66 million Fiverr transactions, a one-hour reply delay cut hiring likelihood by 46%. Experiments found managers read speed as competence, even when quality was lower.

💡 Why it matters
Set evaluation criteria before outreach. Otherwise inbox timing becomes an unspoken hiring metric and can beat evidence of quality.

☕ Coffee talk
Would the 4.85-star candidate lose in your process because they answered after lunch?

---

2️⃣ **Scale does not excuse weak demand**
Bain and NIQ tracked nearly 500 large US consumer brands. The 71 that beat their category every year since 2022 held 8% of 2025 share but produced 44% of net growth.

💡 Why it matters
Scale was not the edge. The winners expanded distribution and sales velocity, while peers leaned mainly on price.

☕ Coffee talk
How many brands still get budget because they are large, not because demand is moving?

---

3️⃣ **Put heat in the operating plan**
WHO and WMO say heat is already hurting worker health and productivity. Their guidance calls for tailored heat-action plans; OSHA adds daily ownership, monitoring and clear escalation.

💡 Why it matters
A heatwave needs a named owner, thresholds, schedule changes, backup capacity and drills.

☕ Coffee talk
Who can slow a shift at 3pm when the heat threshold is crossed, without waiting for head office?
