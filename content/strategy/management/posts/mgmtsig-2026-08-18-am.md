1️⃣ **Train strategists to challenge the machine**
BCG says AI is commoditising analysis. The strategist's edge moves to choosing the right thinking mode and testing the assumptions behind machine output.

💡 Why it matters
More analysis can create false confidence. Put assumption audits, red teams and explicit human overrides into major decisions.

☕ Coffee talk
Who gets 48 hours to argue against the AI-backed recommendation before the money moves?

---

2️⃣ **Let strong people outgrow their job descriptions**
Vanta's new CMO moved across product marketing, brand, communications and revenue marketing over six years instead of leaving for the next title.

💡 Why it matters
Retention gets easier when the best next role can exist inside the company. Scope should move as the person and the business change.

☕ Coffee talk
Which high performer is job-hunting because your org chart still describes the person you hired three years ago?

---

3️⃣ **Treat trust as transformation infrastructure**
McKinsey argues that AI adoption depends on transparency, clear expectations and visible investment in employees, not just better technology.

💡 Why it matters
People slow down when they cannot see what AI changes for their work. Managers need answers before the rollout reaches the team.

☕ Coffee talk
Can your managers explain who gains authority from the new AI workflow and who loses it?
