1️⃣ **Frontline ideas need a route to action**
MIT SMR surveyed 150 hospital frontline workers. More than 80% wanted to innovate, yet fewer than half felt able to do so and 70% rarely or never shared ideas.

💡 Why it matters
Give middle managers the time and authority to turn a useful observation into a small, measured experiment.

☕ Coffee talk
Does the nurse with a better process know who can test it next week?

---

2️⃣ **Make AI argue before it answers**
In a hackathon with nearly 2,000 participants, teams produced a wider range of more innovative ideas when AI challenged duplication instead of simply improving their proposals.

💡 Why it matters
AI should surface competing evidence and demand a first view from the user, not make the first answer easier to accept.

☕ Coffee talk
Could your strategy team defend its first view before the chatbot makes it sound finished?

---

3️⃣ **Schedule leave before crisis schedules it**
DJ DiDonna says roughly two-thirds of the sabbaticals in his research began after a personal or professional crisis. Those breaks cost more and need longer recovery.

💡 Why it matters
Plan eligibility, cover and timing early. Extended leave can test succession and resilience before burnout forces the issue.

☕ Coffee talk
Could one senior leader step away without Slack becoming life support?
