1️⃣ **Do not automate the apprenticeship**
As AI absorbs research, documentation and basic analysis, McKinsey argues that firms must redesign early-career roles, coaching and knowledge systems together.

💡 Why it matters
Removing junior tasks without replacing how judgment is learned creates faster output and a thinner bench.

☕ Coffee talk
Which task did you automate that used to teach someone how the business actually works?

---

2️⃣ **Collaboration needs an off switch**
HBR's latest read on high-performing teams argues that protected individual work matters as much as collaboration. Constant contact can flatten the range of ideas.

💡 Why it matters
Managers should design a rhythm: work alone, compare thinking, then decide. An open channel all day is not teamwork.

☕ Coffee talk
Does your team have quiet time, or just meetings with gaps too short to use?

---

3️⃣ **Local learning can beat early reach**
Research on more than 1,000 tech startups found that firms with more local users before a global platform launch later gained more foreign users.

💡 Why it matters
Early reach is useful only if leaders can read the feedback. A smaller, legible market may teach more than scattered global demand.

☕ Coffee talk
Are you expanding because the product travels, or because the dashboard found another country?
