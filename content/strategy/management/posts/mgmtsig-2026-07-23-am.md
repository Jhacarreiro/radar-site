1️⃣ **Team friction needs a trait map**
Across eight years and hundreds of teams, MIT SMR's authors found that shared traits can disrupt work. One executive team kept reopening decisions because all its members were highly curious.

💡 Why it matters
Before changing roles or replacing a leader, map the personality mix against the behaviour that is blocking the work.

☕ Coffee talk
How many reopened decisions are strategy problems, and how many are the same curious people enjoying another lap?

---

2️⃣ **Do not make employees defend a black box**
In a six-year bank study, AI made loan decisions that officers could not override but still had to justify. Some masked the model's role with plausible explanations; customers noticed the confusion and some left.

💡 Why it matters
Accountability needs access to evidence, a route to challenge the model and regular contact with its developers and users.

☕ Coffee talk
Would your loan officer admit the model makes no sense, or invent a reason that sounds bankable?

---

3️⃣ **Measure the future, not just last quarter**
BCG's 2026 Vitality Index covers more than 3,500 companies and 10 million data points. Firms above the median recorded annual revenue growth 5.2 percentage points higher over the next five years.

💡 Why it matters
Add leading indicators such as R&D momentum, workforce stability and decision friction to the performance review.

☕ Coffee talk
Which number next Monday says whether the core can still grow in three years?
