1️⃣ **CHMP opens the EU access queue**
EMA's CHMP recommended eight new medicines and 18 indication extensions at its May meeting, including Jascayd for IPF/PPF and an oral Wegovy tablet for weight management.

💡 Why it matters
The next bottleneck is EC sign-off, then national price and reimbursement. Europe now has access work to do in fibrosis and obesity.

☕ Coffee talk
How fast do payers price tablets, injections and antifibrotics before the comparison gets messy?

---

2️⃣ **Retatrutide moves the obesity ceiling**
Lilly said TRIUMPH-1 met phase 3 endpoints: retatrutide 12 mg delivered 28.3% mean weight loss at 80 weeks; 45.3% of patients lost at least 30%.

💡 Why it matters
The obesity market is shifting from GLP-1 access to multi-agonist depth, tolerability and sequencing before Lilly files.

☕ Coffee talk
If 30% loss becomes a drug target, how long does "surgery-level" stay a premium category?

---

3️⃣ **LRRK2 takes a Parkinson's hit**
Biogen and Denali said BIIB122 missed primary and secondary endpoints in a 648-patient phase 2b LUMA study and will stop idiopathic Parkinson's development.

💡 Why it matters
The readout weakens the broad LRRK2 thesis and keeps CNS underwriting tied to clinical benefit, not biomarker logic.

☕ Coffee talk
Who still funds Parkinson's biology on mechanism alone after another clean-looking target fails in patients?
